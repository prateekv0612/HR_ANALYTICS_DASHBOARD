# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Overview

This project is an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** built in **Power BI** to analyze employee attrition and identify the key factors influencing workforce turnover. The dashboard provides HR teams with actionable insights to improve employee retention through data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this project is to:

* Analyze employee attrition across different demographic and organizational factors.
* Identify departments, job roles, salary groups, and age categories with high attrition.
* Monitor key HR metrics through an interactive dashboard.
* Support HR managers in developing effective employee retention strategies.

---

## 📂 Dataset

The dataset contains employee-related information, including:

* Employee Demographics
* Age
* Gender
* Education
* Department
* Job Role
* Salary
* Years at Company
* Attrition Status

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Imported HR employee dataset into Power BI.
2. Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
3. Created calculated measures using DAX.
4. Built interactive visualizations for HR metrics.
5. Designed a dashboard with slicers and KPI cards.
6. Analyzed employee attrition patterns and generated business insights.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* Overall employee attrition rate is **16.1%**.
* Employees aged **26–35 years** experience the highest attrition.
* Employees earning **up to 5K salary** account for the largest share of attrition.
* **Laboratory Technicians**, **Sales Executives**, and **Research Scientists** have the highest employee turnover.
* Male employees show higher attrition than female employees.
* Employees with **Life Sciences** and **Medical** educational backgrounds contribute significantly to overall attrition.

---

## ✅ Recommended Actions

* Improve retention initiatives for employees in the **26–35 age group**.
* Review compensation strategies for lower salary bands.
* Conduct exit interviews and engagement surveys for high-turnover job roles.
* Strengthen career development and learning opportunities.
* Introduce targeted retention programs for departments with consistently high attrition.
* Monitor attrition KPIs regularly to evaluate the effectiveness of HR initiatives.

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This dashboard transforms raw HR data into meaningful business insights, enabling HR professionals to identify attrition trends, monitor workforce health, and make informed decisions that improve employee retention and organizational performance.
